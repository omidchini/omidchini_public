# Spades by omidchini - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Spades by omidchini ("the app") is a free card game made by Omid Chini Foroushan. This page explains what the app does with your data.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect personal data, does not send anything to a server and does not share anything with anyone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet: it does not even ask for the internet permission.

## Data kept on your phone

Your games, notes, statistics, lesson progress and settings are saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## Third-party services

The app contains no third-party services or libraries that collect data. Google Play, which you use to install the app, has its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If a future version of the app sends anything off your phone (for example a way to send a game report to the developer), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask you before anything is sent.
